## What the October science adds

**The FIA AGB yield curves tab shows generic aboveground live biomass curves by forest type group and ecological section for the six Northern Forest states.** The curves come from FIA remeasurement intervals with the same method in every state: four candidate forms (CR, CRS, CRD, HG), remeasurement only and joint chronosequence strategies, screens on early rise, floor, and magnitude, a stress test score for decline capable forms, a cap at the type's observed 99th percentile AGB, an extrapolation flag beyond the 99th percentile age, and 200 plot cluster bootstrap draws.

**Maine is the published record (10.5281/zenodo.23406410, October 15, 2026, 60 files).** The Lake States (MI, WI, MN) and NH/VT builds of October 16 live on Cardinal and in `active-projects/perseus/2026-10-27_integration/inputs/` and are not deposited, and the tab labels them as such. The curves match current EVALIDator stock within 2 to 4 percent but reproduce only 60 to 64 percent (Lake States) and about 60 percent (NH, VT) of EVALIDator net growth, because age only population curves smooth over plots growing above their age class. They are the right input for stock and the wrong input for annual flux.

**The RD trend tab gains a thresholds panel from the CONUS relative density breakpoint analysis** (Chivhenge, Weiskittel, Ray, Woodall, D'Amato, and Daigneault, submitted to Forest Ecology and Management; archive concept DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23397937). Supplementary Data S1 gives breakpoints in projected RD for gross growth, net growth, mortality incidence, and mortality severity for 2,020 forest type by EPA Level III ecoregion combinations, each marked as cell fitted and pooled, cell fitted but excluded, or pooled only. The national values are 0.42, 0.34, 0.45, and 0.50. A version 1.2.0 of the archive was built on October 26 as an unsubmitted Zenodo draft, and since S1 is unchanged across versions the concept DOI is the right citation.

**Two further October outputs are recorded here but not wired in.** The fvs-modern internal FIA benchmark tables (19 variants, 433,291 remeasurement conditions, in sample) were packaged for Zenodo on October 19 without a minted DOI. The October 18 reconciliation note found that the manuscript's R² values cannot be regenerated from any table in the fvs-modern repository, so no benchmark number should be quoted in the engine crosswalk until the manuscript tables and the deposit agree. The FVS-ACD 14.3 thin and partial removal pre-registered test (October 19) concerns the Acadian variant and does not change the PERSEUS production engine (recalibrated growth with gompit mortality, decided June 28 and evidenced in 10.5281/zenodo.21027931).

## Open decisions

**Three choices are the maintainer's and block specific steps.** The raster home decides whether `public/raster/` (131 MB of PNG, 191 MB packed in history) moves to object storage with the checked in manifest, to release assets, or stays, and the branch only prepares the move. A server side Paddle checkout bound to the signed in user is the one security item still open before any paid launch. Whether to rewrite history with `git filter-repo` to reclaim the raster blobs is worth deciding once, after the carry items land, and only if clone size becomes a complaint.
